Daytulo privacy

Updated 24 September 2026. Daytulo is operated by Intellingo Ltd.

This policy covers Daytulo, including features in build 50. Feature availability depends on the version you use.

Before you sign in

Your answers to the welcome questions and the first task you enter for the plan preview are stored and processed on your device. These answers and preview task are not sent to Daytulo’s servers.

Your account

We store your sign-in provider identifier, email address when provided, name, subscription status and usage counters to authenticate you, manage access and prevent misuse. Apple or Google verify their respective sign-ins. Email sign-in links are delivered by Resend.

Your plan and calendars

Your tasks, projects and planning preferences, including any area or project you choose as your focus, are stored on your device. Calendar access requires your permission. Daytulo reads calendars you enable and writes events when you use its calendar features.

When you first choose calendars, your default calendar and other writable calendars start as “Busy”, so tasks are planned around their timed events. Read-only shared calendars start as “Show”; subscribed, birthday and holiday calendars start as “Off” and their events are not read. Events marked Free, declined events and all-day events do not block time by default; your explicit blocking choices in Daytulo take precedence. Existing calendar choices are retained. You can change each calendar’s setting at any time or revoke calendar permission in iOS Settings.

Travel time

Travel time is off unless you turn it on. When enabled, Daytulo uses Apple’s Maps and location services to resolve addresses or locations of upcoming events and estimate journey times. Event addresses and journey endpoints are sent directly from your device to Apple. If you also grant location permission, your current location can be used as a journey’s starting point while you use the app. Daytulo does not request background location access.

Daytulo does not send these travel-time addresses, location fixes or route results to its own servers. Its cached locations and journey estimates stay on your device. Apple processes requests under its Maps privacy notice and Location Services privacy notice. You can turn travel time off in Daytulo or revoke location access in iOS Settings at any time.

Reminders, widgets and Siri

With notification permission, Daytulo can schedule a local reminder before your trial ends. You can switch this reminder off in the app. Scheduling and displaying it does not send reminder data to Daytulo’s servers; subscription information is handled as described below.

Widgets and Live Activities show task names and times from a copy of your plan stored on your device. Anyone who can see your Lock Screen may see this information. You can remove widgets or disable Live Activities in iOS Settings.

When you add a task using Siri or Shortcuts, Apple processes your request under its own privacy policy. Daytulo saves the resulting task on your device.

Smart features and imports

When you request smart estimates or import review, relevant task and project descriptions and planning context are sent through our Convex backend to TypeSafe for processing. Only use these features with information you are comfortable submitting. Imported Todoist data is accessed with your authorisation. You can review an import before adding it to your plan. Short-lived review progress, result identifiers and aggregate usage counters support safe retries and usage limits; task descriptions are not written to diagnostic logs.

Subscriptions

Apple handles payment. RevenueCat validates purchases and manages subscription entitlements using an opaque Daytulo account identifier. We do not receive your card details.

Advertising measurement

Advertising measurement is enabled in build 50, subject to the permissions described below. It is not enabled in the initial build 45.

In versions with advertising measurement enabled, Apple’s privacy-preserving attribution system may send an ad network delayed reports about an ad-driven install and milestones such as opening the app, reaching the paywall, starting a trial or becoming a subscriber. These reports do not include your Daytulo account identifier or your task or calendar content.

If you allow tracking through the iOS permission prompt, Daytulo also uses Meta Platforms’ SDK and RevenueCat’s Meta integration to measure which ads lead to app use, trials and subscriptions. Information shared for this purpose includes app activation, a Meta-generated installation identifier, a hashed version of your Daytulo account identifier, device identifiers (including the advertising identifier), IP address, device information, and trial and subscription events. Meta may link this information with information from other apps and websites for advertising measurement. RevenueCat sends trial, purchase and renewal events to Meta, including after the app is closed. Meta’s SDK also processes technical information and crash diagnostics to operate and maintain the integration.

Daytulo starts Meta’s SDK and links device identifiers for this integration only after you allow tracking. RevenueCat’s integration requires tracking authorization before sending App Store subscription events. Declining tracking does not prevent you from using Daytulo or subscribing. We do not send your task text, calendar content, name or email address to Meta through this integration.

You can change your choice in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Daytulo checks permission when it becomes active and before purchase-related measurement; when permission is withdrawn, it stops enabling this measurement and clears the attribution fields it controls in RevenueCat. Withdrawal does not undo data already processed. Meta handles information it has received under its Privacy Policy; you can use Meta’s privacy controls and contact us for privacy requests.

Retention and deletion

Account records are kept while your account exists. You can delete your account from account settings, including the paywall account menu. This removes Daytulo account and usage records and the signed-in account’s local plan and setup drafts on this device. Copies you saved or shared elsewhere and events in your calendar accounts are unaffected. Apple sign-in access is revoked during deletion. Payment records may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat for billing and legal purposes. Account deletion does not cancel an App Store subscription. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Your choices

You can refuse calendar or location access, turn off travel time or trial reminders, disconnect integrations, delete local tasks, or request access, correction or deletion of account data. We do not sell your personal information.
