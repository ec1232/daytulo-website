How can we help?

Email Daytulo support

Tell us what happened and what you expected. In Rhythm, use Share diagnostics to include app and recovery information without task text or account details.

Your saved plan

Stored in your account and on your devices. Export a backup in Rhythm any time. Signing in on another iPhone restores the account copy. If a subscription has ended, the copy already on the phone stays and can still be exported.

Assistants

Connect and remove assistants from Rhythm. The public guide is Connected assistants.

Calendars and reminders

Choose calendars in Rhythm, and choose whether the account stores nothing, busy times, or busy times with titles. Open Daytulo after external calendar changes, then recalculate and publish your updated plan when needed.

Purchases

Use Restore purchases on the paywall with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Manage or cancel renewal in your Apple Account subscriptions. Deleting a Daytulo account does not cancel billing.

Account recovery

If deletion or a save is interrupted, follow the recovery message before reinstalling. Keep any backup until recovery is complete.
