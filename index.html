Is Daytulo the right day planner for you?

It depends. Daytulo suits you if you want an iPhone planner that turns your tasks into a realistic day around your calendar, deadlines and daily rhythm, with scheduling on your device. Optional AI can label tasks with duration, effort and priority, and you can connect Claude, ChatGPT or Muse through its MCP connector. A simple to-do list is a better fit if you only need to write tasks down. A full calendar app is a better fit if you want to replace your calendar rather than plan around it.

What is Daytulo?

Daytulo is an AI daily planner for iPhone that turns your tasks into a realistic day plan around your calendar, deadlines and daily rhythm. The App Store lists it as Daytulo AI Daily Planner.

Where does scheduling run?

Scheduling runs on-device. Optional AI labels tasks with duration, effort and priority.
